# 🚀 EveryPosting: Complete SaaS Growth & Go-To-Market Plan

**Product Name**: EveryPosting  
**Category**: AI Content Repurposing & Multi-Channel Distribution SaaS  
**Target Audience**: Podcasters, YouTubers, Online Coaches, Course Creators & Solo Marketers  
**Pricing**: Free Tier (3 generations/mo) | Pro Monthly ($19/mo) | Pro Yearly ($190/yr) | BYOK Tier  
**Target Goal**: Scale from $0 to $10,000 MRR in 6 months ($100,000 ARR in 12 months)

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Growth Strategy Overview

EveryPosting solves a universal pain point for digital creators: **producing core content is hard, but formatting and distributing it across 5+ social platforms is tedious and time-consuming.**

By leveraging a high-value niche positioning (Podcasters, YouTubers, Coaches) and a low-friction $19/mo price point, EveryPosting is built for rapid product-led growth (PLG) combined with organic creator outbound and affiliate leverage.

### Growth Financial Milestones
- **Milestone 1 (Months 1–2)**: $1,000 MRR (~53 active Pro subscribers)
- **Milestone 2 (Months 3–6)**: $10,000 MRR (~526 active Pro subscribers)
- **Milestone 3 (Months 6–12)**: $30,000+ MRR (~1,580 active Pro subscribers)

## 2. Phase 1: Zero-to-1 Launch & Seed Customers (Months 1–3)
**Objective**: Reach first 100 paying customers (~$2,000 MRR) with $0 ad spend.

### 2.1 Launch Platforms & Community Drop Sequence
1. **Product Hunt Launch**:
   - Prepare high-contrast visuals using official brand colors (`#FF529A` Pink, Dark Obsidian backdrop).
   - Offer a launch promo: "50% off first month with code `LAUNCH50`" or 5 extra free trial generations.
   - Coordinate upvotes with creator networks and Indie Hackers communities.
2. **Indie Hackers & Reddit Showcases**:
   - Posts on `r/SaaS`, `r/podcasting`, `r/Youtube`, `r/Entrepreneur`, and `r/contentmarketing`.
   - Post structure: "I built an AI tool that turns 1 podcast episode into 10 social posts in 30 seconds. Here is what I learned." (Share authentic insights, not hard spam).
3. **Skool & Discord Creator Communities**:
   - Partner with hosts of creator communities (e.g. podcasting, video production, coaching groups).
   - Offer community members free trial upgrades in exchange for honest video testimonials.

### 2.2 Direct Creator Cold Outreach (Hyper-Personalized DMs)
Target: 30 targeted DMs per day on X/Twitter and LinkedIn.

- **Target Profiles**:
  - Podcasters with 1,000–50,000 listeners (visible RSS feeds or Spotify links).
  - YouTubers posting weekly long-form videos (1k–100k subscribers).
  - Active coaches on LinkedIn posting regular text updates.
- **Outreach Script Template**:
  > *"Hey [Name], loved your latest episode on [Topic]! I noticed you didn't share a text summary or thread on LinkedIn/X for it. I built a quick AI tool called EveryPosting that turned your episode transcript into 3 ready-to-post drafts in 20 seconds. Want me to send the drafts over for free?"*

## 6. Retention, Onboarding & Churn Reduction Strategy

To maintain a healthy SaaS business, monthly churn must remain **below 5%**.

### 6.1 Activation & Onboarding Flow
1. **Instant Gratification (<60 seconds)**: On first sign-in, guide users directly to the input box with pre-filled sample text so they can see immediate outputs without typing.
2. **Welcome & Nudge Email Sequence**:
   - *Day 1*: Quick 1-minute video demo of EveryPosting.
   - *Day 3*: Case study on how a podcaster saved 5 hours/week.
   - *Day 6*: Pro feature showcase (Newsletter generation & BYOK flexibility).
   - *Day 14 (For Free Users)*: Discount code / trial expiration reminder.

### 6.2 Plan Retention & Upgrade Triggers
- **Credit Warning Banner**: Display a visual progress bar when users hit 80% of their free credits (2/3 generations) prompting upgrade to $19/mo.
- **Annual Upgrade Incentive**: Offer 2 months free ($190/yr instead of $228/yr) on checkout and account settings.
